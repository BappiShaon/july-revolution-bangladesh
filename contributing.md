# How to Contribute to This Dataset

We welcome contributions! Help us build the most comprehensive database of July 2024 Revolution research.

## 🎯 What We Accept

✅ Peer-reviewed journal articles
✅ Books and book chapters
✅ Dissertations and theses
✅ Research reports
✅ News articles (from credible sources)
✅ Datasets (related to July 2024)
✅ Working papers

❌ Opinion pieces (unless from reputable publications)
❌ Duplicates (we check for these)
❌ Unrelated articles (must be about July 2024 Revolution)

## 📝 How to Submit

### Method 1: GitHub Issue (Recommended)

1. Go to: https://github.com/BappiShaon/july-revolution-bangladesh/issues
2. Click New Issue
3. Fill in:

Title: [Article Title]

Authors: [Names]

Publication Year: [Year]

Journal/Publisher: [Name]

DOI/URL: [Link]

Category: [Choose one]
- Politics & Governance
- Health & Mental Health
- Media & Information
- Human Rights & Law
- Social Movement
- Education
- Other

Summary: [2-3 sentences]

PDF Available: Yes or No

PDF File: [Attach if available]

### Method 2: Email

Send to: syful.au.bd@gmail.com with:
- Article title
- Authors
- Publication year
- Journal name
- DOI/URL
- Category
- 2-3 sentence summary
- PDF attachment (optional)

## ✅ What Happens Next

1. Review (3-5 days) - We verify the article
2. Approval - Added to dataset if valid
3. Credit - Your name added to CONTRIBUTORS list
4. Notification - We'll confirm when added

## 🚫 Duplicate Detection

We check for duplicates using:
- Exact title match
- Same DOI
- Same authors + year + similar title
- Same journal + authors + year

Duplicates will be marked but we appreciate the effort!

## 📋 Categories Explained

Politics & Governance - Government response, democracy, autocracy

Health & Mental Health - Mental health, injuries, public health, trauma

Media & Information - News coverage, disinformation, digital platforms

Human Rights & Law - Legal analysis, crimes against humanity

Social Movement - Protest dynamics, activism, youth movements

Education - Student protests, university impacts

Other - Anything not fitting above
